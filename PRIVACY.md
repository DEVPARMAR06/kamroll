# Kamroll privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026.

Kamroll backs up the photos and videos on your iPhone to a drive you choose. **It does not collect, send or sell any data.** There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and the developer never receives anything from your use of the app.

## What the app reads, and where it stays

| Data | Why | Where it stays |
|---|---|---|
| Photos and videos on your iPhone, with their file names, dates and any location saved inside them | To copy them and sort them into dated folders and trips | Copied to the drive you choose. Nothing is changed or deleted on the iPhone |
| Your iPhone's name, model and iOS version | To show which phone is connected | Shown on screen only |
| The pairing record Apple's software stores on your computer when you tap "Trust" | To talk to your iPhone over USB | Read on your computer, never copied or sent |
| Records about the backup (file list, fingerprints, tags, trips and your edits) | To resume, verify and organise the backup | In the hidden `.photovault` folder on your backup drive |
| Your settings | To remember your choices | In one settings file on your computer (Setup shows its location). The Microsoft Store version keeps it inside the app's private data folder, which Windows removes when you uninstall |

## Network use

Kamroll opens no network port and makes no internet connections. It talks to Apple's USB service on your own computer. If, and only if, you turn on smart tags, it sends small previews of your pictures and trip details to the [Ollama](https://ollama.com) program **running on your own computer** (`127.0.0.1`); addresses of other computers are refused. Place names for trips come from a list built into the app.

## Stores

If you install Kamroll from the Microsoft Store or another store, that store may collect information such as downloads and crashes under its own privacy policy.

## Your control

Your photos and records are ordinary files on your own drives. You can copy, move or delete them at any time. Uninstalling the app does not delete your backup.
